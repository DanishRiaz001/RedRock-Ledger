Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2025

RedRock Regnskap ("we", "our", or "the app") is an accounting application for small businesses. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. We take your financial data seriously and keep it secure.

1. Who We Are

RedRock Regnskap is developed and operated by Danish Riaz. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at danishriaz001@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the accounting service:

  • Account information: Your email address and password (stored as a secure hash — we never see your password in plain text).
  • Company profile: Business name, address, organisation number, VAT number, and bank account details you choose to enter.
  • Financial records: Transactions, journal entries, account codes, and balances you create inside the app.
  • Uploaded documents: Invoice and receipt files (PDF, JPG, PNG) you upload to the Voucher Inbox.
  • AI-extracted metadata: Supplier name, invoice number, amount, and dates extracted automatically from uploaded documents. The original file is never sent to a third-party AI without your action.
  • Device & usage data: Basic crash reports and error logs to help us fix bugs. We do not collect browsing history, location, or contacts.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate the accounting features of the app.
  • To display your financial records back to you and generate reports.
  • To process AI analysis of documents you explicitly submit for extraction.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • We do not sell your data to any third party. We do not use your financial data for advertising.

4. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored on Supabase (supabase.com), a secure cloud database platform hosted on AWS. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Access is restricted to your account only — no other user can view your records.

Uploaded files are stored in Supabase Storage under a path unique to your user ID. Signed, time-limited URLs are used to access files — they expire automatically.

5. AI Document Analysis

When you choose to analyse a document (invoice, receipt), its content is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for text extraction. Anthropic's data handling policy applies to this processing. We send only the document content — no account identifiers. Anthropic does not train models on API inputs by default. See anthropic.com/privacy.

6. Data Sharing

We share your data only with:

  • Supabase — database and file storage provider.
  • Anthropic — only for documents you explicitly submit for AI analysis.
  • Legal authorities — if required by law or court order.

We do not share data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics companies.

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all your records and uploaded files are permanently deleted within 30 days. You may request deletion at any time by emailing us.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict processing.
  • Data portability (export your records).

To exercise any of these rights, email danishriaz001@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

RedRock Regnskap is a business accounting application intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
